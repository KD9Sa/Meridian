About this workspace

A complete, thoughtfully bounded demonstration.

A little context before you explore

Meridian is a fictional revenue operations workspace for Acme Studio. Its reporting snapshot covers June 2026. All customers, payments, and activity are sample records.

Explore with confidence

Navigation, disclosures, native popovers, form validation, and in-page appearance controls work in your browser. Transaction status views show different selections of the supplied sample records.

A static portfolio project

No payments are processed, messages delivered, or account changes saved. Reports do not update live. Appearance and presentation choices apply to the current page.
